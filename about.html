A little about me.

I’m Sacchin, a robotics researcher and generative artist from Madurai.

I build learning systems for physical robots. Currently, I’m a Member of Technical Staff at a stealth company. I completed my master’s at the University of Michigan, working on underwater perception and SLAM.

Away from robots, I make art rooted in Tamil landscapes, temple murals, and the places I call home.
